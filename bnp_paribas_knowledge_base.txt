1. BNP PARIBAS — GENERAL OVERVIEW
============================================================

BNP Paribas is a European leader in banking and financial services with a global footprint. The BNP Paribas Group operates in 64 countries and territories and has more than 180,000 employees.

BNP Paribas describes itself as "the bank for a changing world."

The Group's company purpose is:
"WE ARE AT THE SERVICE OF OUR CLIENTS AND THE WORLD WE LIVE IN."

BNP Paribas supports a broad range of customers:
- Individual customers

- Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs)
- Large corporate clients
- Institutional investors
- Banks

- Real estate services

3. HISTORY OF BNP PARIBAS
============================================================

BNP Paribas was officially established on 23 May 2000 through the merger of:
- BNP, France's leading deposit bank
- Paribas, an international investment bank

The Group's history goes back approximately two centuries. The banks that form the BNP Paribas heritage have been involved in European and international economic and financial development for more than 200 years.

The BNP Paribas historical identity has been shaped by:
- Banking
- Financing economic development
- International expansion
- Mergers and transformations
- Adaptation to economic and social change

Important date:
23 May 2000 = official creation of BNP Paribas.
